Wallpapers for the whole desk.

Still is a wallpaper app for the Mac. It changes your desktop on a schedule, runs one picture across every display you own, and plays live scenes behind your icons.

Coming to the Mac App Store See the live scenes

One picture, across all of them.

Connect a second display and Still offers to run one wallpaper across both, following the arrangement you set in System Settings. With one display, the option is not there at all.

Or a different one on each.

Pick a wallpaper for each display yourself, or let every display work through your collections on its own.

Never blurry

Every wallpaper is checked against the displays connected right now. A picture too small for a Retina display is skipped, not stretched.

On the hour

Change every hour, every three hours, or at a time of day. Changes land on the clock, not whenever you happened to open the app.

Paused until you say

A fresh install changes nothing. Your desktop stays as it is until you press Start.

In the menu bar

Next, Previous and Pause are one click away, without opening a window.

Live scenes, running right now.

This is not a video. It is the same file Still draws behind your desktop icons, running in your browser. Each scene is one small file that can draw and nothing else: it cannot reach the internet or your files.

A catalog we draw ourselves.

Every wallpaper in Discover is made or picked by us, sized for Retina displays, and grouped into collections you can save in one click. No uploads and no ads.

Three tabs. That is the whole app.

Nothing to sign in to and nothing to configure before it is useful.
